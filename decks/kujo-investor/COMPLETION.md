Corrections include current 2026 survey data, explicit component boundaries, mobile reflow, table containment and focus, unique landmarks, thumbnail nested links, missing favicon requests, and a native animation fallback that prevents a stalled transition from leaving a blank locked slide. The final visual pass corrected floating-point percentage formatting. Original failed attempts remain in QA logs; final named reports are authoritative for the delivered artifact.

PackWrite implementation/review prompts were used to structure the work. Loop D executes the actual native build action. The other official loop runs evaluate retained artifacts after assistant research/editing/review. The Bash workflow cannot independently judge visual polish or investment merit: those stages are explicitly assistant self-reviews, not a second reviewer or actual investor endorsement. Each stage is capped at three iterations and two repeated failures; accepted reviews and deterministic gate success are required to stop.
