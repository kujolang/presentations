# Editable financing assumptions
Planning date 2026-11-09. No agreed terms, valuation validation or financial forecast. Edit assumptions.json, then rerun scripts/appendix.py to update machine-readable assets and scenarios.csv, or use the browser calculator and export JSON for review. Canonical model is finance/assumptions.json; browser exports are separate what-if files. Narrative budget text describes the original $6M case and must be reconciled after editing.

$6M allocation: $3.30M engineering (55%), $0.60M infrastructure (10%), $0.60M distribution (10%), $0.60M operations (10%), $0.90M reserve (15%). Operating spend $5.10M / 24 = $212,500/month average. Gross capital / 24 = $250,000 including reserve. Founder base $300K for 24 months, plus illustrative 25% payroll burden = $375K, included in engineering, not added twice. Remaining engineering $2.925M supports, for example, 12.19 engineer-years at $240K fully loaded. That is an average of 6.09 engineers over two years; hiring ramps and cash timing must be modeled before commitments.

Illustrative quarterly non-reserve spend: Q1 $350K, Q2 $450K, Q3 $550K, Q4 $650K, Q5 $700K, Q6 $750K, Q7 $800K, Q8 $850K = $5.10M. Reserve $900K remains. No revenue offsets assumed. Costs vary with location, payroll burdens and compute demand. $3M scope reduces hiring and commercial concurrency; $8M/$10M expand execution capacity, not merely runway promises. Founder compensation held constant across scenarios, not a fixed percentage.

At fixed illustrative $30M post-money, ownership for $3M/$6M/$8M/$10M is 10%/20%/26.67%/33.33%. Actual SAFE conversion, option pools, other convertibles and later dilution can differ. Subsequent 20% dilution changes a 20% holding to 16%. Neither cap nor valuation is negotiated.

Pricing hypotheses: individual open tooling; team $24K/year; enterprise $60K/year, excluding execution pass-through charges. No validated willingness to pay. Target count 2,000 × $24K = $48M annual serviceable scenario; 100 × $24K = $2.4M attainable milestone scenario; 10,000 × $60K = $600M longer-term expansion scenario, not additive to the initial pool. Counts are unsourced planning assumptions, not externally measured market size.
