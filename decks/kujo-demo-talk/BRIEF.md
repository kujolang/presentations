# Morrow Worklab AI research demo

Audience: Social viewers evaluating Kujo Presentations.
Purpose: Demonstrate a survey-driven research report with a dark technology-consulting identity, white exhibit pages, and an explicit fictional data boundary.
Length: Nine slides.
Brand: Morrow Worklab; near-black, vivid green, deep forest, white, gray, bold sans-serif typography, luminous particle imagery.

## Design reference

The visual and structural reference is BCG's “AI at Work: Momentum Builds, but Gaps Remain” presentation published at https://slides.wiki/presentations/bcg-ai-at-work and reviewed on 2026-10-02. The demo adapts the reference deck's dark cover, white survey exhibits, numbered takeaway strip, adoption-by-role bars, time-savings chart, barrier framing, workflow maturity measures, AI-agent findings, and leadership agenda. It does not reproduce BCG or BCG X branding, survey data, copy, proprietary artwork, citations, or research claims.

## Source and truth boundary

Morrow Worklab is fictional. All survey parameters, findings, percentages, markets, role groups, charts, and recommendations are illustrative and were created for this Kujo Presentations demo. They do not represent measured research. The two raster images are original AI-generated assets created for this deck and contain no intended real-world branding.

## Image prompt set

- Cover: an original luminous emerald particle sphere on black, composed on the right with negative space for title typography.
- Leadership close: an original emerald particle canopy across the upper half of a black widescreen canvas.
