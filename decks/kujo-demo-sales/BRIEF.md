# Springline consumer company demo

Audience: Social viewers evaluating Kujo Presentations.
Purpose: Demonstrate a consumer company overview with a portfolio-led visual system that is clearly distinct from the original orange deck and the Vela investor deck.
Length: Nine slides.
Brand: Springline, a fictional food and beverage company; cobalt, cyan, red, white, strong sans-serif display type, saturated lifestyle and product photography.

## Design reference

The visual and structural reference is the PepsiCo presentation published at https://slides.wiki/presentations/pepsico and reviewed on 2026-10-02. The nine-slide selection begins with the reference deck's slide 3 and draws from slides 3, 4, 5, 8, 13, 16, 24, 29, and 35 for its mix of section cover, company overview, portfolio, capability investment, consumer trends, strategic priorities, dayparts, responsible growth, and long-term framework. The demo adapts those presentation patterns rather than reproducing the reference's copy, logos, proprietary brand assets, photography, or factual claims.

## Source and truth boundary

Springline Consumer Company is fictional. All copy, portfolio architecture, metrics, targets, and strategy are illustrative and were created for this Kujo Presentations product demo. The four raster images are original AI-generated assets created for this deck; they contain no intended real-world brands or trade dress. Every quantitative statement is labeled illustrative and must not be represented as historical results, guidance, or a commercial offer.

## Image prompt set

- Lifestyle section cover: diverse adults sharing colorful unbranded drinks and snacks in a bright contemporary living room, with blue negative space for typography.
- Product portfolio: original unbranded snacks, sparkling drinks, functional beverages, and breakfast foods on a pale ice-blue studio background.
- Daypart montage: breakfast, midday work meal, outdoor afternoon snack, and evening dinner as four distinct scenes.
- Responsible growth: an agronomist inspecting crops near a modern food production facility with recyclable packaging and produce crates.

## Unresolved facts

None. The deck is an explicitly fictional design demonstration.
