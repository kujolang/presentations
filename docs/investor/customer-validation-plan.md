# Customer validation — proposed 90-day plan
Owner: founder until a named customer-development lead is hired. No interviews, outreach, quotes, pilots or testimonials have been completed by this session. Outreach copy below is prepared, not sent.

Recruit 12 interviews: 7 platform/engineering leaders running coding agents, 3 engineering managers responsible for review/CI and 2 security or compliance stakeholders involved in repository controls. Favor recent real incidents and identifiable budget authority; include teams happy with existing tools.

Ask: What was the last agent-created change you investigated? Walk through the incident and tools. Which evidence was missing? How long did reconstruction take, and who participated? Show the actual artifacts if authorized. What was the business consequence? Which review/audit obligations were real? What do you use now? What does it cost? Who owns the budget? How would a purchase get approved? What would make you refuse this service? Only after discovery, show the demo and ask what it fails to solve. Do not lead with an expected savings claim or preferred price.

Notes template: date; participant role and consent; company segment without unnecessary personal data; incident date; exact permitted quote; observed artifact vs recollection; reconstruction time and denominator; current workaround; budget/process; objections; willingness-to-pay evidence; next consented step. Classify each entry observed / participant-reported / hypothesis; never promote a quote into verified economics. Keep private interview records outside public deck assets.

Design partner offer: a scoped four-week evidence-review pilot on one approved repository, onboarding and weekly review. Qualify actual agent usage, budget owner, meaningful recurring reviews, data permission and willingness to share measured outcomes. No production customer data before service readiness gates. Price discussion: proposed $500/month team subscription; any free pilot and conversion date documented explicitly. Success: complete evidence for agreed runs, repeat weekly reviewer use, measured reconstruction effort against the team's own baseline and an explicit paid renewal decision. Record failures and unchanged outcomes too.

Days 1–30: 12 interviews; at least 4 recent costly incidents with inspectable evidence and 3 qualified partners. Days 31–60: onboard 3 scoped pilots after readiness checks, compare evidence completeness and review effort, record support/compute costs. Days 61–90: seek 2 paid conversions, four-week active use and at least one renewal decision; if not reached, stop hiring expansion and revise the wedge. These are proposed gates, not completed milestones.

Outreach draft: “I’m researching how engineering teams review agent-driven repository changes. Could we spend 25 minutes walking through a recent investigation and the tools you already use? I’m interested in what worked as much as what was missing. With your permission I can then show a short evidence workflow.” Follow-up draft: “Here is my factual summary of the incident and gaps you described. Please correct it. May we agree a scoped evaluation with explicit data boundaries and a review date?” Do not send without authorization.
